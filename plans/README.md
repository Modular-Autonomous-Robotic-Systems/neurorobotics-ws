# Plans Index

This directory is the **committed change ledger** for the Neurorobotics Workspace (`NRT_WS`) — one document per feature/task produced by the `nrt-RandD-workflow` (investigate → plan → validate → implement). Each file records the motivation, the changes made, usage, and the validation strategy, and stands as durable history of how the project evolved. Together with `context/` and `ARCHITECTURE.md` these files form the project's knowledge base / digital twin.

> **Maintenance rule:** every non-trivial task writes or updates a plan here (see `CLAUDE.md §4`). During development a plan may carry investigation and validation detail. On completion it is rewritten as a clean change-record, and that rewrite is a **subtraction of review and design-iteration artefacts only, never a summarisation**. Every code snippet, design decision, rejected alternative with its reason, backwards-compatibility analysis and validation check is retained in full, and what goes is the revision markers, the reviewer-facing wording, the feedback rounds and the resolved uncertainty. Durable findings additionally move into `context/`. The full rule is in the `nrt-RandD-workflow` skill, Phase 6. Submodules may keep their own `plans/`; when Claude is invoked at a submodule root, that submodule's `plans/` is used.

## How to use this index

1. Skim the table below to see prior/ongoing work relevant to your task.
2. Open the linked plan for the full change-record before re-deriving history from `git`.
3. After finishing a task, add or refresh the plan file and its row here.

| [sitl-clock-synchronisation.md](sitl-clock-synchronisation.md) | Put AirSim, ArduPilot SITL and the SLAM stack on one clock, so that camera and inertial stamps share a time domain; and record the hardware/HITL architecture that does not follow from it | **IMPLEMENTED 2026-07-28 for SITL** — container-side validation outstanding and catalogued in the plan's §8. Camera frames carry AirSim's `SteppableClock` and `/ap/imu/experimental/data` carries ArduPilot's real-time clock, and the two are rate-locked but origin-independent, leaving a fixed ~6.13 s offset that hangs Basalt outright when its bounded IMU queue fills and blocks the compute node's single-threaded executor. The fix connects two mechanisms that already exist and are simply not wired: `airsim_ros_pkgs` can publish its simulator clock but does so on the private `~/clock`, and ArduPilot master compiles a `/clock` DataReader into every SITL build that overrides every `/ap/*` header stamp. One `use-sim-time` launch argument (default `False`, `choices=["True","False"]`, so no existing caller changes) turns on `publish_clock`, remaps `/airsim_node/clock` to `/clock` with a scoped `SetRemap`, binds ArduPilot's `--use_sim_time`, and sets the `use_sim_time` parameter on the SLAM compute node and the recorder while deliberately refusing it on both supervisor controllers — with §4.6 recording the exact steps and watchdog changes for granting it to them later. §6 is a full ArduPilot time architecture: the two-layer `micros64()`/`AP_RTC` model with flow charts for stamp production and `rtc_shift` establishment, why `AP_DDS_CLOCK_SUB_ENABLED` is SITL-only and semantically wrong on hardware, the unused `uxr_sync_session` NTP handshake vendored in the micro-XRCE-DDS client, and a ranked table ending in a common UTC reference (GPS/PPS or NTP/PTP) with MAVLink `SYSTEM_TIME` plus `BRD_RTC_TYPES=2` as the GPS-denied fallback. Records why the MAVLink route cannot work *in SITL*, four independent reasons, and why `synthetic_clock` is a no-op. See [context/sitl-time-synchronisation.md](../context/sitl-time-synchronisation.md). |
